TASK 2 – JOB SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES
Task 2.1 – Model
Input: A finite set of jobs; each job has a unique ID, positive integer deadline, and non-negative profit. Each job takes exactly one slot.
Output: A feasible slot-to-job schedule and total profit.
Objective: Maximise the sum of profits of scheduled jobs.
Constraints: At most one job per slot; a job in slot t must satisfy t <= its deadline; each job is scheduled at most once.
Decision: Which jobs to accept and in which available slot to place each accepted job.
Required information: Job deadline, profit, slot occupancy, and maximum deadline.

Task 2.2 – Greedy hypothesis
Process jobs in descending order of profit and place each job in the latest free slot at or before its deadline. This prioritises high-profit jobs while preserving earlier slots for jobs with tighter deadlines.

Task 2.3 – Pseudocode
ALGORITHM JobSequencing(Jobs)
INPUT: Jobs with ID, deadline, profit
OUTPUT: Schedule and total profit
BEGIN
    SORT Jobs by descending profit
    maxDeadline ← maximum deadline among Jobs
    FOR slot ← 1 TO maxDeadline DO
        Schedule[slot] ← EMPTY
    END FOR
    totalProfit ← 0

    FOR each job in Jobs DO
        slot ← MIN(job.deadline, maxDeadline)
        WHILE slot >= 1 AND Schedule[slot] is not EMPTY DO
            slot ← slot - 1
        END WHILE

        IF slot >= 1 THEN
            Schedule[slot] ← job.ID
            totalProfit ← totalProfit + job.profit
        END IF
    END FOR

    RETURN Schedule, totalProfit
END

Task 2.4 – Desk-check
Jobs sorted by profit: A(2,80), C(2,60), D(3,40), B(1,35), E(1,25).
Initial slots: [1: empty, 2: empty, 3: empty].
A: latest free slot <= 2 is 2. Place A in slot 2. Profit = 80.
C: latest free slot <= 2 is 1. Place C in slot 1. Profit = 140.
D: latest free slot <= 3 is 3. Place D in slot 3. Profit = 180.
B: slot 1 is occupied; no earlier slot exists. Reject B. Profit = 180.
E: slot 1 is occupied; no earlier slot exists. Reject E. Profit = 180.
Final schedule: slot 1=C, slot 2=A, slot 3=D. Total profit = 180.

Task 2.5 – Challenge
Change only D's profit from 40 to 90. Prediction: the order changes because D becomes the highest-profit job; its placement is expected to use slot 3, while A and C can still occupy slots 2 and 1.
New order: D(3,90), A(2,80), C(2,60), B(1,35), E(1,25).
D goes to slot 3; A goes to slot 2; C goes to slot 1; B and E are rejected. The schedule by slot remains C, A, D, but total profit becomes 230. The prediction that the priority decision changes is correct; the occupied slot arrangement happens to remain the same.
